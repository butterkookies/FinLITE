## Complete Project Background (For Reference)

**1.1 Project Context**
FinLITE is a computerized financial management system designed to benefit the League of Information Technology Enthusiasts (LITE). It is used to securely manage the organization's financial records by facilitating core processes such as logging income and expenses, monitoring discrepancies, and automating financial reporting. Implementing FinLITE offers LITE a significant upgrade from its current operational workflows, driving greater productivity and accuracy.

**Organizational Background**
LITE is a recognized student organization for BSIT students at the Pambayang Dalubhasaan ng Marilao (PDM), located in Abangan Norte, Marilao, Bulacan. Guided by club advisers Ms. Kimberly Dawn Jatulan and Ms. Krizia Mae Genovia, the organization comprises officers, committee chairpersons, and BSIT students. They regularly conduct meetings in the 2nd-floor consultation room of the PDM building, as they lack a permanent office.

**The Current Manual Process**
Currently, LITE does not utilize any computerized system or even basic spreadsheets. The treasurer or auditor manually logs transactions (expenditures and income) onto random pieces of paper, lacking a dedicated logbook. This unstructured method leads to missing crucial details (e.g., exact transaction dates, handlers of funds). Consequently, tracing historical transactions becomes highly difficult, unorganized, and time-consuming, making financial reporting the organization's primary challenge.

**Statement of the Problem**
Based on an interview with the LITE President (who served as auditor and treasurer from AY 2024–2026), tracking transactions is a major difficulty. The lack of proper tracking and uniformity in record-keeping leads to severe disorganization and financial discrepancies, often unnoticed until end-of-term financial reporting. Retrieving past records is tedious for officers and advisers, while manual computation causes significant delays and puts data accuracy at high risk.

**Proposed Solution**
To resolve these operational bottlenecks, the proponents propose the development of FinLITE. Designed to replace manual inefficiencies, FinLITE streamlines transaction management via a secure, user-friendly interface for fast data access. The system features a modular architecture for tracking income and expenses and integrates an A.I. chatbot. This AI tool will assist users in retrieving specific data and automating financial report generation, ultimately ensuring productivity and financial data integrity for LITE.
