SYSTEM PURPOSE

What is FinLITE Built For?

FinLITE bridges the gap between high-friction corporate accounting software and unstructured manual record-keeping for LITE.

1. Digitize Transactions
Replaces loose paper receipts and chat messages with a centralized, secure digital ledger that tracks cash collections, GCash payments, and personal advances.
2. Automate Liquidation
Compiles verified ledger records into audit-ready Word (.docx) reports matching institutional college standards in 1 click, eliminating arithmetic and formatting errors.
3. Intelligent Co-Pilot
Provides an embedded zero-hallucination AI Chatbot that instantly answers budget, expense, and reimbursement questions directly from live ledger records.
PROBLEM STATEMENT

Main Financial Problems of LITE

Field interviews across successive administrations revealed four critical bottlenecks in current manual workflows:

1. Personal Advance Vulnerability (Abono)
Officers front personal money for event materials. Thermal paper receipts fade, crumple, or get lost, causing unliquidated personal losses.
2. GCash Commingling & Unlogged Fees
Digital tournament payments mix with officers' personal mobile wallets. Unrecorded ₱15–₱20 withdrawal fees create persistent cash shortages.
3. Cash Count Discrepancies & Freezes
Physical banknote and coin counts are performed months late. When math discrepancies occur, all club spending and activities immediately freeze.
4. 6-Stage Signatory Paper Gridlock
Manual Word reports rejected at later stages (Director/Dean) require re-editing, re-printing, and restarting the entire physical wet-ink signature chain from scratch.
SYSTEM FEATURES · PART 1

Dual-Vault Tracker & Advance Management

Eliminates commingled personal funds and protects officers who front personal capital.

Dual-Vault Separation
Strictly separates liquid assets into a Physical Cashbox Vault (Program Director's room) and a Digital GCash In-Transit Vault.
Cash-Out Fee Logger
Automatically logs ₱15–₱20 third-party withdrawal fees as legitimate operational expenses so officers never pay out-of-pocket.
Abono Dual Tracking
Tracks both the original payer and the claimant, paired with photo receipt staging and a 7-day countdown badge.
SYSTEM FEATURES · PART 2

Interactive Cash Denomination Counter

Solves math errors by auditing physical bills and coins against the theoretical ledger in real-time.

Digital Keypad
Dedicated inputs for all Philippine banknotes (₱1,000 to ₱20) and coins (₱20 to ₱1 plus centavos).
Real-Time Variance Engine
Automatically calculates: Counted Cash - Theoretical Ledger Balance. Instantly flags Balanced, Overage, or Shortage.
Shortage Justification
Generates a formal Cash Shortage Justification Report for dual-adviser sign-off (modeled after the ₱161 precedent) to prevent spending freezes.
SYSTEM FEATURES · PART 3

1-Click Institutional DOCX & AI Co-Pilot

Automates formal college report compliance and provides natural-language budget intelligence.

1-Click Institutional DOCX Export
  • • Format Compliance: Calibri 11pt, 1.0-inch margins, and borderless financial tables.
  • • 6-Stage Signatory Chain: Programmatically formats signature blocks for Treasurer, Auditor, President, Dual Advisers, Director, and Dean.
  • • In-App Preview: Live interactive preview with dynamic Carbon Copy (CC) toggle before downloading.
Zero-Hallucination AI Chatbot
  • • Grounded in Live Data: Answers questions strictly using current Supabase transaction records.
  • • Instant Answers: Officers and advisers query remaining budgets, pending abono, and category totals in plain language.
  • • Streaming Responses: Fast, conversational interface integrated into the side drawer.
TECH STACK & SELECTION RATIONALE · PART 1

Frontend & Database Architecture

Selected for performance, modern development velocity, and strict data security.

Next.js 15 & React 19
Why selected: Provides high-speed server rendering, robust route handlers, and modern component architecture for a responsive single-page experience.
Tailwind CSS v4
Why selected: Enables strict design tokens, clean hairline borders, and rapid UI styling without bloated CSS stylesheets.
Supabase (PostgreSQL)
Why selected: Delivers enterprise-grade relational integrity, cloud photo storage for receipts, and verified Row-Level Security (RLS) to block unauthorized access.
TECH STACK & SELECTION RATIONALE · PART 2

Math Engine, Document Generation & AI

Selected to eliminate math drift, guarantee institutional formatting, and deliver intelligent assistance.

Centavo Integer Math
Why selected: Completely eliminates JavaScript IEEE-754 floating-point rounding errors by calculating all balances in whole integer centavos.
Python DOCX Engine
Why selected: Allows programmatic manipulation of Word XML to guarantee exact 1-inch margins, borderless tables, and signatory blocks required by the college.
Google Gemini API
Why selected: Provides fast, reliable natural language understanding with zero hallucination by injecting live ledger summaries into the system prompt.
SYSTEM WORKFLOW

Basic Flow of the FinLITE System

A streamlined 4-step pipeline from transaction entry to final wet-ink sign-off:

STEP 1
Entry & Receipts
Officer logs cash/GCash collection or records an abono advance with uploaded receipt photos.
STEP 2
Ledger & Vaults
System automatically routes funds into Cashbox or GCash In-Transit vaults with centavo precision.
STEP 3
Physical Audit
Officers input banknote and coin counts into the digital counter to verify zero discrepancy.
STEP 4
1-Click Sign-Off
System compiles the audit-ready Word liquidation report ready for the 6-stage wet-ink signature chain.
