COLLEGIATE OPERATIONAL CONSTRAINTS

Four Real-World Operational Realities

1
No Physical Office: Officers lack a permanent headquarters, requiring mobile, cloud-accessible bookkeeping from anywhere.
2
Single Metal Cashbox: Physical money is locked inside a shared metal box stored in the Program Director's office.
3
High-Stress Turnover: Passing cash custody between officers requires an immediate, manual piece count of every bill and coin.
4
Strict Segregation of Duties: College rules strictly separate the roles of the Counter (Treasurer) and the Verifier (Auditor).
FIELD RESEARCH FINDINGS

Five Core Operational Problems

1
Abono Liabilities: Officers pay club expenses out-of-pocket and suffer personal financial loss when paper receipts are lost or damaged.
2
Micro-Transit Leakage: Tricycle and jeepney fares lack official printed receipts, creating unaccounted cash discrepancies.
3
Turnover Discrepancies: Unrecorded transactions cause unexplained shortages that officers must cover out-of-pocket for clearance.
4
Slow Manual Computations: Hand-calculating cashbox balances without automated formulas hides errors until the end of the semester.
5
Signatory Routing Bottlenecks: Even a tiny manual arithmetic error restarts a rigid 6-stage physical liquidation signing process.
DATABASE SELECTION RATIONALE
Architecture Choice

Why PostgreSQL on Supabase over MongoDB?

1
Money Can Never Be Out of Sync: When cash is spent or recorded, the receipt and the ledger update together at the exact same instant—it can never be "half-saved" or delayed.
2
Receipts Never Get Disconnected: Relational links lock receipt photos to their specific transaction and officer so proof can never be lost or orphaned.
3
Exact Centavo Math: PostgreSQL prevents decimal rounding glitches, ensuring every single centavo in the cashbox matches the final financial liquidation report.
4
Secure Cloud Backups: Automatic cloud connection pooling, continuous SSL encryption, and daily backups protect club funds from data loss.

DATA INTEGRITY & VALIDATION DEFENSES
Core Defenses

Two-Tier Data Integrity: Database Engine & Server Schemas

Tier 1: PostgreSQL Engine-Level Constraints:
• CHECK (amount > 0): Makes negative or zero ledger entries physically impossible at the database engine level.
• UNIQUE constraints on email, denomination codes, and active semester indicators.
• ON DELETE RESTRICT: Prohibits cascading deletions that would orphan financial records.

Tier 2: Server-Side Zod Schemas & Dynamic RBAC:
• Enforces strict MIME types (jpeg/png/webp) and 1MB size limit on receipts.
• Dynamically evaluates capability flags (can_record_inflows, can_reconcile_cash) before query execution.
• Masks internal PostgreSQL stack traces from the browser client.
