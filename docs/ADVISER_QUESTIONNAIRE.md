⚠️ WHY WE ARE BUILDING FINLITE FOR YOU:
Managing student organization finances is often one of the most stressful responsibilities in student leadership—and historically, faculty advisers and student officers end up carrying unfair personal burdens:

1. Advancing Personal Money ('Abono') — Funding urgent tokens, student meals, and event materials out-of-pocket, only to wait weeks for reimbursement or lose track of crumpled paper receipts.
2. Digital Payment Bottlenecks (GCash) — Digital collections pooling into personal mobile wallets, mixing with personal funds and leaving cash-out withdrawal fees unaccounted for.
3. The High-Stress Liquidation Cycle — A single decimal discrepancy or misaligned table margin causing multi-page reports to be rejected by the Department or Dean, forcing you to reprint and chase 6 physical signatures all over again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
💡 OUR VISION FOR FINLITE (KEY CAPABILITIES):
FinLITE is not a complicated corporate accounting tool. It is an agile, user-friendly digital financial co-pilot built around how PDM actually operates:

✦ Real-Time Treasury Transparency (Centralized Fund Tracking)
A unified digital ledger that records all inflows, registration fees, and micro-disbursements in real time—giving advisers 24/7 visibility over cash balances and eliminating lost, loose paper logs.

✦ Zero Lost 'Abono' (Automated Reimbursement Queue)
A transparent Accounts Payable queue that clearly tracks who advanced personal money, stores photo attachments of receipts/slips, and ensures prompt, verified cashbox reimbursement.

✦ Dual Cash & GCash Liquidation Tracking
Monitors both physical cashbox reserves and digital mobile wallet inflows with tracked cash-out fees, completely eliminating mental math errors.

✦ 1-Click Official Institutional Reports
Instantly compiles verified records into the exact Microsoft Word (.docx) layout mandated by the College of Computer Studies (Times New Roman, formal transmittal headers, borderless tables, and the 6-stage wet-ink signatory block).

✦ Protected Annual Leadership Turnover
An interactive denomination counter (₱1,000 down to ₱1) that ensures a clean, certified balance handoff from outgoing to incoming officers without surprise deficits or stressful officer liabilities.
