Part I • Design System Foundations

Functional Color Roles in FinLITE

Every color in a financial system serves a designated communicative duty. FinLITE separates identity, data readability, and spatial boundaries into 5 disciplined tiers:

1. High-Contrast Text
--active-text
Deep, desaturated green shades (600–900). Ensures minimum 7:1 WCAG AAA contrast ratio on pure white backgrounds and light tints for amounts and headers.
2. Primary Accent
--active-primary
The distinctive brand signature. Powers primary buttons, squircle identity emblems, active segmented controls, and focal action triggers.
3. Mid Transition Shade
--active-mid
Vibrant bridge tone used in directional gradients, active progress indicators, and trend badges (+12.4% net revenue).
4. Surface Tints & Borders
--active-tint / --border
Light pastel green washes (50–200) for the flat Cashbox card, table row active states, pill backdrops, and delicate hairline separators.
