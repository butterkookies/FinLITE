**Title:** 	FinLITE: A Web-Based Financial Assistant System for the League of Information Technology Enthusiasts

**1.1 Project Context**  
FinLITE is a computerized financial assistant system designed to benefit the League of Information Technology Enthusiasts (LITE). It is used to securely manage the organization's financial records and is built to perform core processes such as recording income and expenses, monitoring discrepancies, and generating financial reports. Implementing FinLITE presents a strong opportunity for LITE to upgrade its operational processes and become more productive.

The League of Information Technology Enthusiasts, also known as LITE, is recognized as the student organization for BSIT students at the Pambayang Dalubhasaan ng Marilao (a local college located in Abangan Norte, Marilao, Bulacan). Currently, the organization is guided by its club advisers, Ms. Kimberly Dawn Jatulan and Ms. Krizia Mae Genovia. The organization is composed of the two club advisers, an executive board (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Auditor), 1st to 4th year level representatives, specialized sub-committee chairpersons (Arduino, Photography, Singing, Dancing, E-sports, Multimedia, Cybersecurity, Programming, and Networking), and students from the BSIT program. While they do not have a permanent office, they regularly conduct their meetings in the faculty room located on the second floor of the PDM building.

**The Current Manual Process**  
For every expenditure, the club's treasurer or auditor manually logs the transaction details onto a piece of paper. The organization lacks a dedicated logbook for expenses, which often leads to missing crucial details such as the exact transaction date and the name of the person who handled the funds. A similarly unstructured process is used for recording the organization's income. Currently, LITE does not utilize any computerized system or even a basic spreadsheet to manage its financial transactions. This manual approach directly contributes to their primary challenge: generating financial reports. Because the written logs are scattered and unorganized, tracing the organization's historical transactions becomes highly difficult and time-consuming.

**Statement of the Problem**  
The proponents conducted an interview with the President of LITE, who served as the auditor and later as the treasurer for the academic years 2024 to 2026\. During their tenure, they encountered multiple difficulties in tracking transactions. This lack of proper tracking sometimes led to financial discrepancies that were only noticed when finalizing the financial reports at the end of the term. Transactions are usually logged on random pieces of paper, which results in severe disorganization and a lack of uniformity and formality in their record-keeping. Consequently, retrieving past transaction records becomes a tedious task for the auditor, treasurer, and club advisers. Furthermore, the manual computation of these scattered records causes significant delays and puts the accuracy of the financial data at high risk.

**Proposed Solution**  
To address the operational challenges encountered by the League of Information Technology Enthusiasts (LITE), the proponents propose the development of FinLITE, a comprehensive financial assistant system. Designed to eliminate the inefficiencies of manual record-keeping, FinLITE streamlines transaction management through a secure, user-friendly interface that ensures fast and direct access to financial data. The system features modular architecture for tracking income and expenses and integrates an intelligent AI financial assistant. The system’s main users are limited only to the LITE club advisers, president, treasurer, and auditor to maintain exclusivity and to avoid mismanagement and security issues. This tool assists users in retrieving specific data and automating the generation of financial reports, thereby enhancing both productivity and accuracy for the organization.
