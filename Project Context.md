**Title:** 	FinLITE: A Web-Based Financial Assistant System for the League of Information Technology Enthusiasts

**1.1 Project Context**  
FinLITE is a computerized financial assistant system designed to benefit the League of Information Technology Enthusiasts (LITE). It is used to securely manage the organization's financial records and is built to perform core processes such as recording inflows and disbursements, monitoring physical cashbox balances, tracking personal advances (*"abono"*), and generating institutional financial reports. Implementing FinLITE presents a strong opportunity for LITE to upgrade its operational processes, eliminate administrative bottlenecks, and enhance custodial accountability.

The League of Information Technology Enthusiasts, also known as LITE, is recognized as the student organization for BSIT students at the Pambayang Dalubhasaan ng Marilao (a local college located in Abangan Norte, Marilao, Bulacan). Currently, the organization is guided by its designated faculty club advisers, Ms. Kimberly Dawn Jatulan and Ms. Krizia Mae Genovia. The organization is composed of the two club advisers, an executive board (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Auditor), 1st to 4th year-level representatives, specialized sub-committee chairpersons (Arduino, Photography, Singing, Dancing, E-sports, Multimedia, Cybersecurity, Programming, and Networking), and BSIT students. While they do not possess a dedicated physical office, official consultations occur at the adviser's station in the second-floor faculty room, while the physical small metal cashbox is permanently secured inside a locked drawer within the Program Director's (PD's) Room. Under institutional policy, the cashbox never leaves campus; all off-campus purchases are funded via personal advances (*"abono"*) and reimbursed on campus. Furthermore, LITE collects no mandatory student membership dues; its operating funds derive strictly from tournament registration fees, ad-hoc batch merchandise sales (100% upfront payment), and booth space rentals and sales commissions during Institutional Club Week.

**The Current Manual Process**  
For expenditures and out-of-pocket personal advances (*"abono"*), the club's treasurer or auditor manually logs transaction details onto loose pieces of paper or unstandardized sticky notes. The organization lacks a dedicated, structured logbook for expenses, which frequently leads to missing crucial metadata such as the exact transaction timestamp, receipt attachments, the name of the person who advanced personal funds, and the authorized claimant. An unstructured process is similarly used for recording organization inflows (event fees, merchandise collections, and booth commissions) and digital GCash collections pooled into personal accounts. Unrecorded ₱15–₱20 cash-out fees, delays exceeding the 7-day receipt submission cutoff, and latent math errors compound over the term. Currently, LITE does not utilize any computerized system or basic spreadsheet to manage its physical-to-digital financial reconciliation. This manual approach directly contributes to their primary challenge: compiling final financial liquidation reports and surviving the rigorous six-stage sequential wet-ink signatory chain. Because written logs are scattered and disorganized, tracing historical transactions, verifying claims, and conducting denomination counts becomes highly chaotic and time-consuming.

**Statement of the Problem**  
The proponents conducted comprehensive field interviews with the President of LITE (who previously served as Auditor and Treasurer for academic years 2024 to 2026), corroborated by formal survey assessments with Faculty Club Adviser Ms. Krizia Mae Genovia. During their tenures, custodians encountered severe operational vulnerabilities:
1. **Personal Financial Exposure & Abono Delays:** Late submission of receipts (past the 7-day activity window) and missing proof force advisers and officers to shoulder unreimbursed out-of-pocket costs personally.
2. **Digital Co-Mingling & Ledger Drift:** Personal GCash pooling causes temporal lag, personal/club fund commingling, and unaccounted withdrawal fees.
3. **Custodial Stress & Face-Value Turnover:** Storing funds in a small metal cashbox without real-time ledgers forces outgoing officers to pay out-of-pocket (*"abono"*) at year-end to satisfy the strict face-value turnover protocol.
4. **Discrepancy Paralysis:** Undetected arithmetic errors and unlogged micro-disbursements trigger institutional spending and activity freezes upon audit discovery.
5. **Administrative Routing Gridlock:** Manual report formatting flaws or minor decimal errors trigger immediate rejection by the BSIT Program Director or College Dean, restarting the entire six-stage physical wet-ink approval chain from scratch.

**Proposed Solution**  
To address the operational challenges encountered by LITE, the proponents propose the development of FinLITE, a web-based financial assistant system. Designed to eliminate the inefficiencies of manual record-keeping, FinLITE streamlines transaction management through a secure, role-gated interface restricted to institutional `@pdm.edu.ph` accounts. FinLITE features:
- **Segregation of Duties & Dual Adviser Multisig:** Role-gated controls enforcing independent Treasurer custody, Auditor verification, and mandatory Dual Sign-Off (2-of-2 joint approval) by both Faculty Club Advisers.
- **Personal Advance ("Abono") Queue:** An auditable Accounts Payable state machine tracking creditor metadata, enforcing the ₱1,000 prior-approval threshold, validating 7-day receipt deadlines, and supporting partial payouts.
- **Interactive Denomination Counter & Discrepancy Auditor:** A digital bill and coin keypad (₱1,000 down to ₱1) providing real-time cashbox-to-ledger reconciliation, automated shortage freeze warnings, allowable minor shortage declarations (₱161 historical precedent), and emergency overage buffering.
- **1:1 Institutional DOCX Master Exporter:** Pixel-perfect report compiler faithfully reproducing College of Computer Studies layout standards with instant report unlocking for rapid in-place re-export upon administrative review.
- **Deterministic AI Financial Co-Pilot:** A zero-hallucination conversational assistant parsing English and Taglish queries via deterministic database aggregations.
- **Turnover & Accounts Payable Carryover Engine:** Guarantees certified face-value custodial handoffs and carries legitimate unpaid Abono claims into next year's opening balance as certified liabilities.
